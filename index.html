AACE


Autonomous • AI • Cybersecurity • EV

Powering Innovation Towards a Smarter and Safer Automotive Future.

Vision

To create a collaborative platform where academia, industry, and policymakers converge to discuss the transformative technologies shaping the mobility landscape — from autonomous driving and AI-enabled decision systems to cybersecurity and sustainable electric mobility.

Objectives

  • Advance knowledge and collaboration in autonomous vehicles, AI, cybersecurity, and electric mobility.
  • Explore integration of intelligence, connectivity, and security in next-gen vehicles.
  • Provide hands-on learning through workshops and interactive sessions.

About the Event

AUTOWN’25 aims to bring together academia, industry, and policymakers on a collaborative platform to explore the transformative technologies driving the future of transportation. Focusing on autonomous vehicles, artificial intelligence, automotive cybersecurity, and electric mobility, the event seeks to foster innovation, knowledge exchange, and sustainable mobility solutions. Through expert talks, technical sessions, and hands-on workshops, participants will engage with emerging tools, simulation platforms, and real-world applications, gaining both strategic insights and practical experience in building intelligent, secure, and sustainable mobility ecosystems.
