Vision

To create a collaborative platform where academia, industry, and policymakers converge to discuss the transformative technologies shaping the mobility landscape — from autonomous driving and AI-enabled decision systems to cybersecurity and sustainable electric mobility.

Quick Facts

  • Dates: Feb 20–21, 2026
  • Venue: BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus
  • Tracks: Autonomous • AI • Cybersecurity • EV

Objectives

  • Advance knowledge and collaboration in autonomous vehicles, AI systems, cybersecurity, and electric mobility.
  • Explore integration of intelligence, connectivity, and security in next-gen vehicles.
  • Provide hands-on learning through workshops, competitions and interactive sessions.

About the Event

AUTOWN’25 brings together academia, industry and policymakers to explore technologies driving the future of transportation — autonomous vehicles, AI, automotive cybersecurity and electric mobility. Expect keynote talks, technical sessions, hands-on workshops, and competitive events: Drone & Robo Racing, Project & Poster presentations.
